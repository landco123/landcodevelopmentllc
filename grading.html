What a Typical Grading Scope Can Include

Every property is different. Some jobs need a simple driveway touch-up. Others need clearing, rough grading, drainage correction, gravel prep or pad preparation before the site is ready for the next step.

Typical Work Items

  • Walk the site and identify runoff, access, elevation and soft areas
  • Clear or open the work area when needed
  • Rough cut, shape and move material for the correct grade
  • Blend slopes, transitions, shoulders and usable areas
  • Prep driveways, pads or yard areas for gravel, seed, sod or the next trade
  • Leave the site cleaner, shaped and easier to maintain

Landco Standard

The goal is not just to make dirt look flat. The goal is to leave the property with better access, better water movement, a cleaner working surface and a grade that makes sense after the equipment leaves.

That is the difference between machine work and development-minded grading.

Why Grading Jobs Fail

  • No runoff plan before moving material
  • Low spots left behind that hold water
  • Water accidentally directed toward a house, driveway or structure
  • Driveway base or gravel prep skipped
  • Surface left rough instead of ready for the next phase
  • Clearing, drainage and grading treated as separate problems

Grading Questions

A few common questions property owners ask before scheduling grading, driveway prep, house pad prep or drainage grading work.

Do you handle small grading jobs?

Yes. Landco Development LLC handles driveway grading, yard leveling, pad prep, drainage grading, gravel driveway prep and smaller site preparation jobs when the scope fits the equipment and the site.

Can grading help with drainage?

Yes. Proper grading can help move water away from low spots, driveways, foundations and areas where runoff is causing problems. Some projects may also need French drains, swales, catch basins or additional drainage correction.

Do you build houses?

No. Landco Development LLC focuses on land development, grading, clearing, drainage, driveway prep, pad prep and site preparation. We prepare and shape the land around the project, but we do not market ourselves as a homebuilder.

Do you provide land clearing and grading together?

Yes. Landco Development LLC can clear brush, open up access, remove small growth and grade the area so the property becomes more usable.

What information should I send for a grading quote?

Send the project location, project type, photos or video if available, what needs to be corrected and whether the job involves a driveway, yard, pad, drainage problem, clearing or full site prep.
